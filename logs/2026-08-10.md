## Research: personal "your genres" subgraph from listening history

**What we did.** Owner asked whether users could connect Spotify and see a subgraph
of their listened genres plus similar ones to branch into. Researched it and wrote
`docs/research/listening-history-personalization.md`; added the feature to
`docs/future.md` under Deferred from v1.

**The reason.** The naive design ("create an account and connect Spotify") collides
with both the access reality — Spotify dev mode is capped at 5 allowlisted users and
extended quota needs a 250k-MAU organization (per the 2026-08-04 research) — and the
project's no-backend/no-accounts architecture. Worth establishing what IS possible
before the idea calcified as impossible or got built the wrong way.

**How it was solved.** Verified live (2026-08-10) that ListenBrainz's stats API
serves a user's top artists with MBIDs, unauthenticated, with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`
— and ListenBrainz auto-imports Spotify listens, making it the bridge. Confirmed via
the Feb 2026 changelog that Spotify's own `/me/top/*` endpoints survived (viable only
as a ≤5-user personal mode). Conclusion: feasible with no backend and no accounts —
ListenBrainz intake (MBID-exact) plus a client-side Spotify GDPR-export upload
(name-matched), a new build-time artist→genre reverse index from stage 4's existing
candidates, branch-out suggestions from the fusion/influence edges already in
`graph.json`, and `localStorage` persistence. One scoped exception to "network calls
at build time only" (api.listenbrainz.org, user-initiated). Docs-only change — no
diagram update.
